Login with browser

Sign in with your normal browser, then paste the OpenCode auth cookie value.

  1. Click Open OpenCode and sign in with Google.
  2. Press F12 in the browser.
  3. Open Application > Cookies > https://opencode.ai.
  4. Find the cookie named auth.
  5. Copy only its Value and paste it below.
